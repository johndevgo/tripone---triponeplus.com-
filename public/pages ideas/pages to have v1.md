# TripOne+ public content inventory — v1

This is the maintained public-site coverage map. The typed source of truth is
`src/content/resources.ts` plus `src/content/supplemental-resources.ts`; the
rendered hub is `/resources`, and every article is rendered at
`/resources/[slug]` with metadata, breadcrumbs, structured data, related links
and a conversion path. Content must remain useful and evidence-based. Keyword
stuffing, fabricated claims and near-duplicate doorway pages are prohibited.

## Core commercial and trust pages

- `/` — tourism website builder and operations platform
- `/features` — website, booking, catalogue, SEO and operations capabilities
- `/growth-services` — optional tourism growth support
- `/templates` — shared tourism theme system
- `/pricing` — launch offer and future annual plan
- `/resources` — guides, SEO, conversion, operations and comparisons
- `/privacy`, `/terms` — legal and privacy information
- `/login`, `/signup`, `/forgot-password`, `/reset-password` — account journeys

## Editorial quality gate

Every public page must answer a distinct search intent, use one descriptive H1,
cover the relevant tourism entities naturally, link to the most useful next
step, include descriptive image alternative text where an image conveys
meaning, and provide unique title and description metadata. Pages should be
expanded only when there is useful information to add—not to meet an arbitrary
keyword-density target.
